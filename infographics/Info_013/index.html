An Alien Mind: what OpenAI's Chief Scientist is warning about

Jakub Pachocki (September 6, 2026): AI is grown more than designed, a product of scale we don't fully understand.

  • Goal alignment is not value alignment: keep human values even when no one is watching.
  • Chain-of-thought monitoring is eroding; activation monitoring is needed.
  • Defensive AI justifies scaling, never a reckless race.
  • Recursive self-improvement: keep humans in the loop.
  • Proposal: make safety bars mandatory and enforce them through third parties.
