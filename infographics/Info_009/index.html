LinkedIn publication text

AI Agents vs. Agentic AI: what's the difference?

I compared what the primary sources say about the two terms. The uncomfortable conclusion: there is no consensus definition.

🔎 What I found

  • Only the taxonomy by Sapkota et al. (2025) treats the terms as distinct categories: agent = module for a specific task; agentic AI = coordinated specialized agents with persistent memory.
  • Anthropic separates workflows (predefined paths) from agents (the LLM drives the process).
  • OpenAI defines an agent as a system that acts independently and treats “agenticness” as a degree, not a category.
  • Google bets on layered architecture and the A2A protocol; MCP and AGENTS.md moved to the Agentic AI Foundation (Linux Foundation).

⚠️ The flip side

  • Multi-agent systems can use about 15× the tokens of a chat (Anthropic).
  • The MAST study mapped 14 failure modes in 3 categories.
  • OWASP published a Top 10 of risks for agentic applications.
  • Platforms shift fast: OpenAI shut down Agent Builder and Evals in Jun 2026.

✅ What to do

  1. Start with the simplest solution.
  2. Test end to end and by subtask before granting autonomy.
  3. Keep people in high-risk actions.
  4. State the definition you adopt.
