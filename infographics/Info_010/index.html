LinkedIn publication text

Will more than 40% of agentic AI projects be canceled by the end of 2027?

That is what Gartner predicted on June 25, 2025. I built a 43-slide infographic to separate what holds from what does not.

What the primary source says

  • It is an analyst forecast, not a measured result; the method is not disclosed in the press release.
  • The poll of 3,412 attendees cited in the text measures investment intent and is not the basis of the 40%.
  • Gartner itself projects that 33% of enterprise software will include agentic AI by 2028.

What the evidence shows

  • RAND (2024): organizational and data causes weigh heavily; the “80% fail” figure is a third-party estimate.
  • MIT NANDA (2025): the “95%” refers to generative AI pilots and comes from a preliminary report.
  • McKinsey (August 2026): 40% of large companies now scale agents (27% in 2025), but only 37% see some EBIT.
  • Gartner (April 2026): only 17% of organizations have deployed agents.
  • MAST study: even after tuning, a multi-agent system reached 40.6% accuracy (it started at 25%).

Conclusion: the direction is plausible; the number is not verifiable. 40%, 80% and 95% measure different things and should not be added together.

How to change the trajectory: clear value before the pilot, the right tool for each task, measure reliability (pass^k), human validation, data and infrastructure, cost under control, and proof of autonomy from vendors.
